Horizontal: simulated seconds · Vertical: speed (m/s). Dashed reference: 30 g effective acceleration is not a speed limit.
Model: requested EC/s = drive count × acceleration setting × throttle. Reactor output and stored battery cover demand; battery charges from surplus reactor output, and discharges only for shortfall. Available EC scales acceleration proportionally. All drives are assumed aligned with identical settings. Units of EC are gameplay units, not joules. Acceleration displayed in g-equivalent (÷ 9.80665), not occupant loading. Coasting continues after EC depletion. Nothing here is based on live KSP telemetry. MIT-licensed companion to ksp-surge-drive.
